Controls

Left drag places blocks, right drag on blocks erases, right-click an empty cell for the Hand tool (drag to move around; right-click the ship or pick a block to build again), and Esc deselects the block. Mouse wheel or pinch zooms. W A S D or the arrow keys move around (hold Shift to go faster); so do the Hand tool (H), Space + drag, middle drag and the minimap. Keys 1–0 pick blocks, Q shows all firing arcs. Line (L): drag from one point to another for a straight line of blocks (hold Shift for straight or 45°). The nose points right.

Every weapon can only fire where its shot won't pass through your own ship: hover a gun (or turn on Arcs) to see where. Guns that stick out cover more. The heavy laser looks out from the middle of its 10×10 unit, and its blocks explode when destroyed, wrecking your own ship around it.
