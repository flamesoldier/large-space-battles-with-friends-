Controls

Left drag places blocks, right drag erases. Scroll zooms, middle drag or Space + drag pans (two fingers on touch). Keys 1–0 pick blocks. The nose points right.

Every weapon can only fire where its shot won't pass through your own ship: hover a gun (or turn on Arcs) to see where. Guns that stick out cover more. The heavy laser looks out from the middle of its 10×10 unit, and its blocks explode when destroyed, wrecking your own ship around it.
